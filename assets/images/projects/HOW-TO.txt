PROJECT SCREENSHOTS GO HERE
Save your pictures in this folder with these names (or change the paths in script.js):
  project-01-thumb.png    picture shown on the project card
  project-01-screen1.png  picture shown inside the project details
  project-01-screen2.png  (optional) another detail picture
  project-02-thumb.png ... and so on
Tips: no spaces in filenames, keep each picture under about 1 MB.
