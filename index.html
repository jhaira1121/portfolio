Keeping It Fresh

This portfolio is a living scrapbook. My plan for keeping it up to date:

  • New projects: add each finished project (description, tools used, screenshots) soon after completing it.
  • New certificates: upload every new certificate or award once I receive it.
  • Skills: review and update my skills list at the end of every semester.
  • Content check: proofread the text and test all links and the contact form at least once a month.
  • Improvements: keep improving the design, speed and accessibility based on feedback.
